💰 US Staffing Gross Margin & Markup Calculation

Every submission and placement automatically computes gross margin spread using this industry formula:

Gross Margin ($/hr) = Bill Rate - Pay Rate

Margin % = ((Bill Rate - Pay Rate) / Bill Rate) * 100

• Example REQ-IND-101 at TCS America:

• Bill Rate: $110.00/hr | Pay Rate: $85.00/hr (Rajesh Kulkarni C2C)

• Gross Profit Spread: $25.00/hr ($4,000.00 / month on 160 billable hours)
